LeadTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. About LeadTracker

LeadTracker is a proof-of-concept application designed to receive and display leads submitted through Meta Lead Ads.

2. Information We Collect

The application may process information submitted through Meta lead forms, including names, email addresses, and phone numbers.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to demonstrate lead reception, storage, and display within the LeadTracker application.

4. Data Storage

Lead information is stored in a Supabase database. Access is restricted through the application's authentication and database security controls.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by the services used to operate this proof-of-concept, including Meta and Supabase.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Lead information is retained only as needed for the application's testing and demonstration purposes. For questions or deletion requests, contact us using the email address below.
